Privacy, in plain language

Effective 28 August 2026

Your learning data stays on your device

Contexts, meanings, sources, review history, and voice recordings are stored in your browser using IndexedDB. Context Recall Cards has no account system, cloud sync, advertising, or analytics. The app does not upload this learning data.

Microphone access

The app requests microphone access only after you choose Start recording. Leaving the screen stops the active microphone track. Recording does not clear your context draft. Saved recordings stay in this browser.

Exports

When you explicitly export a backup, the downloaded JSON file can contain your sentences, review history, and voice recordings. You control that file. The app asks for confirmation before creating it. Do not share it unless you intend to share those contents.

Existing licenses

If you restore an existing license, the app sends its token to the Sociobot billing API. A verification result and timestamp stay in this browser for up to one day. The app never receives card details.

Deleting your data

Delete individual contexts from the library. Clearing this site's storage in browser settings deletes all contexts, recordings, history, and the locally stored license. Export a backup first if you want to retain them.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@sociobot.in.
